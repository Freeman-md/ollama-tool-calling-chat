# Supporting skills

Resolve the skills through the current agent's installed catalog. On the original Codex host the Haidrrrry skill is under `~/.codex/skills/haidrrrry-remotion-motion-graphics`, and the other two are under `~/.agents/skills/`. Codex may display Shotcraft as `video-shotcraft:video-shotcraft` because of its upstream plugin metadata. Open the selected skill's SKILL.md before using its guidance. If a supporting skill is absent, continue with the local production notes and state what was unavailable. Install or refresh only when needed and authorized.

| Skill | Responsibility | Open when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Haidrrrry Remotion Motion Graphics](https://github.com/haidrrrry/claude-remotion-skill) | Remotion scene construction, easing, layers, audio cues and render/inspect cadence | Implementing, rendering or editing a Remotion film |
| [Remotion Product Motion](https://github.com/hamgarian/remotion-product-motion) | Readable product interaction, projected framing, deterministic UI, audio evidence | Staging product UI, overlapping camera moves, or measuring a render |
| [Video Shotcraft](https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft) | Specific shot recipes, matched demo code and sound-design methods | An identified handoff needs a recipe, or the job explicitly asks for its template or pipeline |

For a Shotcraft recipe, resolve the name and variant from its gallery index, then read the card and its matching demo. For example, Line Carry keeps a moving focal point in view by coordinating camera travel with path progress. Extract that relationship when useful. The example's poster, styling and timeline belong to the example.

## Resolve overlaps

- Use the brief to decide whether a scene moves or holds. Haidrrrry's stagger, surface treatment and micro-motion are useful for entrances; Product Motion's reading hold takes precedence where the viewer must understand a result.
- For a standalone motion study use Shotcraft as a shot library. Its full-film mode selector, Ink Press, Workbench, export and publishing steps apply only when that pipeline or deliverable is requested. The orchestration brief already selects autonomous production by default.
- Use one editorial timeline. Choose one timing model and one audio cue list so render, motion and sound stay aligned. Keep interpolated camera tracks separate from discrete screen content and audio.
- Use existing product UI for demonstrations of the real product. Create editorial UI for conceptual stories and describe that distinction accurately.
